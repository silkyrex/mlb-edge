# Glossary

**ERA** -- Earned Run Average. Runs a pitcher gives up per 9 innings. Under 3.00 = ELITE. Over 4.50 = FADE.

**FIP** -- Fielding Independent Pitching. What ERA *should* be based only on strikeouts, walks, and home runs. FIP much higher than ERA = pitcher has been lucky, ERA will rise. FIP lower = unlucky, ERA will fall.

**ERA LUCKY** -- FIP is 0.75+ higher than ERA. Pitcher looks better than they are. Hurts ERA-based picks; K picks less affected.

**ERA UNLUCKY** -- FIP is 0.75+ lower than ERA. Pitcher looks worse than they are. Strengthens K Higher picks.

**xFIP** -- Expected FIP. Like FIP but uses league-average home run rate instead of actual HRs allowed. More stable than FIP over small samples.

**WAR** -- Wins Above Replacement. How many wins this player adds vs a replacement-level player. Negative WAR = below replacement; reinforces FADE grade.

**ERA-** -- ERA relative to league average. 100 = average. Below 100 = better than average (e.g. 62 = elite). Above 100 = worse than average.

**WHIP** -- Walks + Hits Per Inning Pitched. Batters reaching base per inning. Lower is better.

**K/9** -- Strikeouts per 9 innings.

**K/BB** -- Strikeout-to-walk ratio. Higher = better control.

**ELITE / mid / FADE** -- Pitcher grades. ELITE = ERA ≤ 3.00. FADE = ERA ≥ 4.50. mid = in between. Bet *with* ELITE pitchers (K Higher, ERA Lower), *against* FADE pitchers (K Lower, ERA Higher).

**Lean signal** -- Noon Discord message. OVER = expect high scoring. UNDER = expect low scoring. AWAY/HOME = one offense is significantly better. Drives pick direction.

**BLOCKED** -- A pick that fails a hard rule. Appears in its own section in analyze output, never in TOP PICKS.

**H+R+RBI** -- Hits + Runs + RBIs combined. Underdog's main batter composite stat.

**IL** -- Injured List. Active IL = pick is BLOCKED. Returned from IL within 7 days = -10 score penalty (rust risk).

**vs RHP / vs LHP** -- Batter performance against right-handed vs left-handed pitchers. Always use the split matching today's starter's arm.

**xAVG / xwOBA** -- Expected stats based on how hard the ball was hit. Real avg much higher than xAVG = lucky, regression risk. Real avg much lower = unlucky, could improve.

**wRC+** -- Weighted Runs Created Plus. Offense run production vs league average, park-adjusted. 100 = average. 148 = 48% above average (elite).

**Multiplier** -- Underdog payout. 1.05x on $10 pays $10.50. Below 1.00x means the market disagrees with that direction -- counts against the pick score.

**Bullpen** -- Relief pitchers after the starter exits. Bullpen ERA matters for H+R+RBI picks in later innings.

**Days rest** -- Pitcher days since last appearance. Short rest (≤3d) = fewer Ks, worse command. Extra rest (≥6d) = possible first-inning rust.

**HP Umpire** -- Home plate umpire. Accuracy and error rate from UmpScorecards. Tight zone = fewer called Ks for pitchers. Expanding zone = more.

**2FA** -- 6-digit code texted when logging into Underdog. Required every browser session.
